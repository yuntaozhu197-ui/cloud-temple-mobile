The source describes a small Wooden Fish / Mokugyo temple block from Kohya-san,
Japan, played with its original cloth-covered beater and recorded with an
AKG 414 microphone. The MP3 file is distributed unmodified. In-browser playback
may trim leading silence and adjust level while preserving the recorded timbre.
